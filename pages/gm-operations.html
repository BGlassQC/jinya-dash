Definitions & attribution

On time: preparation start → item marked ready, within its dish standard. Dish Average: arithmetic mean across all preparations. By Dish includes only dishes averaging above their standard, with at least 20 preparations; ranked by average time over standard. Top 5/10 shows up to that many qualifying dishes. Dish comparisons by employee also use arithmetic mean preparation time. Avg time over: excess beyond standard for late items only. All times are min:sec.

Chef ownership assumes a verified station assignment at preparation start. Multiple cooks or unknown ownership must be shown as shared or unassigned in production.

Hourly periods group items by preparation-start hour across the selected dates. Chef comparisons require similar dish mix and workload. Wait to start is a separate queue measure and is not included in the preparation standard.
